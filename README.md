# Global Sales, Profit & Margin Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyse global sales performance, profitability, and profit margins across products, countries, business segments, and time.

The project uses a sales dataset prepared in Excel with assistance from Copilot in Excel. The dashboard focuses on turning the data into clear KPIs and business insights.

## Key Skills Implemented

- **Excel & Copilot:** Data inspection, cleaning assistance, and preparation of the dataset.
- **Power BI:** Data import, modelling, and report development.
- **DAX & KPI Development:** Measures for Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin, COGS, and Units Sold.
- **Data Visualisation:** Trend analysis, segment and product contribution, and country-level comparisons.
- **Interactive Reporting:** Slicers for Product, Country, Segment, Date, and Year.
- **Business Analysis:** Comparing sales with profitability to identify strong-performing and underperforming areas.

## Key Data Insights

- **Overall performance:** The business generated approximately **118.73M in sales** and **16.89M in profit**, with **101.83M in COGS**.
- **Overall profit margin:** The dashboard reports a profit margin of approximately **14%**.
- **Government segment:** Generated the largest share of sales, approximately **52.5M**, representing around **44.2%** of total sales.
- **Enterprise segment:** Generated approximately **19.61M in sales** but recorded a loss of around **0.61M**, highlighting that higher sales do not always mean higher profitability.
- **Small Business segment:** Contributed approximately **42.43M in sales**, making it another major source of revenue.
- **Product profitability:** Paseo was the leading product by profit, contributing approximately **4.8M**, or **28.4%** of total profit.
- **Monthly performance:** October recorded the highest monthly profit at approximately **3.4M**, followed by December at approximately **2.7M**.
- **Country comparison:** The dashboard enables comparison of sales and profit across countries, helping identify differences in revenue generation and profitability.

*Values are approximate and reflect the dataset and dashboard filters shown.*

## Learning Outcomes

This project helped develop a practical understanding of:

- Preparing and validating data before using it for analysis.
- Using Copilot in Excel to assist with data-cleaning tasks.
- Creating meaningful KPIs and understanding the relationship between sales, costs, profit, and profit margin.
- Writing and using DAX measures for business calculations.
- Selecting suitable visuals to communicate trends, comparisons, and contribution.
- Using slicers to explore data across multiple dimensions.
- Interpreting business performance beyond revenue, especially by identifying segments with low or negative profitability.
- Converting dashboard results into clear, data-supported business observations.

## Conclusion

The dashboard shows how sales, costs, and profitability can tell different stories about business performance. While the Government segment contributes the highest sales, the Enterprise segment operates at a loss despite generating substantial revenue. Product and monthly comparisons also help highlight where profit is concentrated.

The key learning from this project was not just creating visualisations, but understanding how to use data, KPIs, and interactive reporting to identify meaningful business insights and support better decision-making.
